This is the web demo version of the game.

(Emulator project page)

HOW TO PLAY

Hold 'Space' key for 2 seconds to start/restart game.

Press left and right arrow keys to move player.

Press 'Space' key to jump.

Move to the left to get points, avoid falling into the water.
