(Emulator project page)

HOW TO PLAY

Hold 'Space' key for 2 seconds to start/restart game.

Press up arrow key to change game mode.

Press down arrow key to toggle hard mode.

Press left and right arrow keys to move duck.

Repeatedly tap 'Space' to flap your wings and fly.

Collect bread for points, and avoid rockets/explosions to stay alive.
